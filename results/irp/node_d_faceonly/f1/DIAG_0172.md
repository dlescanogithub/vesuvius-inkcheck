# Why the two models barely agree on PHerc0172 (F1, segment 20250926145427, w084)

Post-hoc diagnosis, CPU only, done after F1 was scored. **It does not change the F1 verdict (REJECTED)**, and nothing
here was pre-registered. A = Reader V3, B = dense_native 16k, same as F1. "IoU" means top-3 % IoU (`inkcheck.node_d_v2.top_iou`).
No scroll data or images are committed. Scripts and raw numbers are in `diag_0172/`. Paths in them point to the author's box.

## 1. 0172 comes from a different scan

| segment | CT volume | window CT mean ± sd (valid px) | CT p3–p97 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PHerc0172 pos0 / pos1 | 7.91 µm, **53 keV**, no 1.2 m propagation | 154 ± 26 / 157 ± 27 | 114–206 |
| other 5 segments (10 tiles) | 8.64–9.37 µm, 1.2 m, 113–116 keV | 75–111 ± 34–48 | 25–35 to 148–189 |

All three 0172 segments used in this repo (w082, w083, w084) are on this volume. On 0172 the CT is brighter, its range
is compressed, and it has less contrast.

## 2. A saturates on 0172; B does not

| | 0172 (2 tiles × 2 faces) | other 10 positive tiles |
|---|---|---|
| A median | 150–167 | 65–104 |
| A share of pixels > 128 | 0.56–0.67 | 0.00–0.26 |
| B median | 89–107 | 75–132 |
| share of pixels within ±3 of A's top-3 % threshold | 0.070–0.083 | 0.004–0.064 |

The same pattern appears in the earlier v2 run on 0172 w082/w083: A's depth-0 median was 110–139 there, against 55–97 on
the other v2 segments.

## 3. Not a registration or orientation error

- Searching shifts of ±64 px: the best cross-correlation is within 0.04 of the zero-shift value (fwd 0.63→0.63, 0.53→0.53).
- The identity orientation beats flips, the 180° rotation and the transpose.
- Valid fraction is 1.00 on both tiles.

- Neither smoothing the maps (σ 2–8 px) nor breaking ties with jitter raises the 0172 IoU at q = 0.03.
- So the disagreement is in the fine detail, the strokes, not in the large-scale layout.

## 5. Causal test: change only the CT intensity distribution

- **Setup.** I re-ran both checkpoints on CPU with villa `infer` at HEAD 39a8cf3, using the same flags as F1
  (`--direction both`, 17 window layers). The margin was 128 px instead of F1's 384 px. Each input was histogram-matched,
  quantile by quantile, to another tile's CT.
- **Reproduction check.** Raw 0172 pos0 on CPU against the F1 GPU maps: r = 0.96–0.99 for A and 0.84–0.88 for B.

| input | IoU fwd / rev | face asymmetry | rule outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0172 pos0, raw (F1 GPU, 384 px margin) | 0.003 / 0.005 | 1.72 | NOTHING |
| 0172 pos0, raw (CPU, 128 px margin) | 0.019 / 0.009 | 2.11 | NOTHING (IoU < 0.10) |
| 0172 pos0 → 0009B pos0 histogram | 0.141 / **0.608** | 4.32 | FLAG (on the *rev* face) |
| 0172 pos0 → 0841 pos0 histogram | 0.086 / 0.004 | 20.6 | NOTHING (IoU < 0.10) |
| 0841 pos0, raw (F1 GPU, 384 px margin) | 0.394 / 0.244 | 1.61 | NOTHING |
| 0841 pos0, raw (CPU, 128 px margin) | 0.386 / 0.133 | 2.91 | FLAG |
| 0841 pos0 → 0172 pos0 histogram | 0.277 / 0.056 | 4.92 | FLAG |

After remapping 0172 to look like 0009B, A stops saturating: its median falls from 140–150 to 70–75 and its share of
pixels above 128 from 0.53–0.59 to 0.04–0.20.

## Reading

1. The near-zero agreement on 0172 comes mainly from the input CT distribution of the 53 keV scan. On it, A saturates
   and partly follows CT brightness, so A's top 3 % sits on a flat plateau that B does not share. Remapping intensities
   alone moves the best-face IoU from 0.02 to 0.61.
2. The result is not stable, though:
   - It depends on which reference histogram is used (0.61 against 0.09).
   - The face that agrees can switch.
   - Under the 0009B remap, the 0172 top-3 % overlap forms 2 large blobs (3 011 and 6 260 px) rather than strokes.
3. The face-only outcome is also sensitive to the inference context. For 0841 pos0, the rev-face IoU drops from 0.244 to
   0.133 and the outcome flips from NOTHING to FLAG between the 384 px (GPU) and 128 px (CPU) margins. Margin and device
   were not separated.
4. Both points support the F1 verdict: face asymmetry is not a robust ink signal across scans. This is post hoc, with
   n = 1 tile per arm.
5. A cheap guard follows from this. Report A's saturation (median and share of pixels > 128) and plateau ties at the
   top-q threshold next to any agreement test. `inkcheck.node_d_v2.saturation_check` does this as a warning only; it
   does not change any verdict.

Run with its default settings over all 56 F1 maps (14 regions × 2 models × 2 faces), `saturation_check` flags 5 of them:

- the 4 A maps on 0172, with share > 128 of 0.555–0.672 and plateau ties of 0.070–0.083
- 1 B map, 0841 pos0 fwd, with share > 128 of 0.534 and plateau ties of 0.034. Here the two models still agree (IoU 0.394).

A flag is therefore a reason to look closer, not proof that a map is broken. A high share above 128 together with a large
plateau-tie share is what marks the 0172 case.
